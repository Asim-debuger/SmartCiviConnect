Public FAQ page with an expandable accordion of platform questions and answers.

**Accordion behavior**
- Six predefined question-answer pairs about complaints, tracking, employee tracking, registration, verification, and notifications.
- Only one question is open at a time; clicking a question toggles its answer.
- Uses `ChevronDown` icon with rotation animation for open/closed state.

**Components used**
- `PageHero` for the page header.
- `Container` for centered max-width layout.

**Data**
- All questions and answers are static inline data.

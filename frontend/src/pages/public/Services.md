Public Services page describing SmartciviConnect's civic operation capabilities.

**Service cards**
- Eight services: Complaint Management, Emergency Reporting, Workforce Management, Live Tracking, Department Coordination, Analytics & Reports, Citizen Communication, and Job & Workforce Portal.
- Each card includes an icon, title, description, and bulleted benefits list.

**Components used**
- `PageHero` for the page header.
- `Container` for layout.
- Lucide icons: `ClipboardList`, `Siren`, `Users`, `Radio`, `Briefcase`, `BarChart3`, `MessagesSquare`, `BellRing`.

**Data**
- All service content is static inline data.
